Current Research

Dr. Dutta currently contributes to SERE-IoV, a project on secure and resilient Internet of Vehicles in hybrid radio-frequency and optical-wireless intelligent transportation systems at United Arab Emirates University. The work studies AI-assisted anomaly and cyberattack detection, device trust, adaptive physical-layer security, and their integration into a multi-layer framework for prototype evaluation.
