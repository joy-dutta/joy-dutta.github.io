Biography Summary

Joy Dutta is a Computer Science and Engineering educator and researcher working on intelligent and secure connected systems. He is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Information Systems and Security, College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, United Arab Emirates University, Al Ain, UAE. His core research focuses on agentic edge intelligence for trustworthy, autonomous, and resource-aware decision-making in intelligent infrastructure.

He earned his Ph.D. in Engineering (Computer Science and Engineering) from Jadavpur University and completed his B.Sc. in Physics (Honours), Post-B.Sc. B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering, and M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Calcutta. Before joining UAEU, he spent four years as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Khalifa University, where he contributed to funded research on resilient edge computing, eHealth, and hybrid electric-vehicle charging infrastructure.

His teaching experience includes Assistant Professor appointments at KIIT University and Sister Nivedita University. He has co-supervised MSc research and undergraduate projects, using laboratories and project-based learning to connect computing concepts with practical problems. Dr. Dutta is a Senior Member of IEEE, has more than 1,000 citations, an h-index of 18, an i10-index of 20, and paper awards from IFIP IoT 2023 and IEEE OCIT 2022.
